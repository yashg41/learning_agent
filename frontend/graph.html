Reading the map
Colour means you have met the concept; grey means you have not, and the fill deepens as you master it. A violet ring marks a concept whose prerequisites you already know — those are your next steps.

Prerequisites flow left → right; each lane is one track. Edges appear when you hover a concept. Dashed edges cross tracks — those are the bridges that make one subject unlock another.
